Scope

The workshop is organised as part of EDUCARE ("The Broad Impact of Early Childhood Education and Care"), a research project on how early childhood education and care affects children and their parents. We welcome papers on education and care for children aged 0 to 5, and are particularly interested in the first three years of life. Topics include:

  • Effects on children: development, health and later educational outcomes, including gaps by family background
  • Effects on parents: mothers' employment, earnings and career progression, the child penalty and the division of care within the household
  • Access and allocation: admission lotteries, priority rules and who gets a place when places are rationed
  • Design and expansion of public childcare: universal provision, quality of care (child-to-staff ratios, staff qualifications) and public versus private provision

Format

Each paper is presented in 25 minutes, followed by 5 minutes from an assigned discussant and 5 minutes of Q&A. The working language is English.

Submission

To be considered, submit your paper as a PDF through the form below by 31 January 2027. Full papers are preferred; extended abstracts with the main results will also be considered.

If you work on these topics but do not have a paper ready to present, you can still take part: use the same form to apply to act as a discussant.

Authors chosen to present will be notified by the end of February 2027. Please share this call with colleagues who may be interested.
